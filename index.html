About NotesBazaar

Making study resources easier to discover and share.

NotesBazaar is a student-focused educational platform designed to help learners discover, access, and share useful study materials. From handwritten notes to chapter-wise summaries and top resources, it brings everything into one place so students can learn with confidence.

Share Your Knowledge

Help other students learn by sharing your notes.

  1. Open NotesBazaar and navigate to the Upload section.
  2. Select the PDF or image files you want to share.
  3. Enter the note title, subject, class, board, and optional chapter name.
  4. Review your information and tap Upload Note.
